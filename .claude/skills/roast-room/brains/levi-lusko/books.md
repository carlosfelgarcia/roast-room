# Books: Levi Lusko

Eight of his books are discussed in the corpus, plus a children's version of his first. Book text was not fetched; each entry is how he teaches the book in interviews, podcasts, and sermons, summarized in my own words. Years are as he or the sources give them. Blessed Are the Spiraling is his ninth book (S010), so at least one book is not covered here.

## Through the Eyes of a Lion (2015)
- **Core thesis:** Written after his five-year-old daughter Lenya died of an asthma attack five days before Christmas 2012. Her corneas gave sight to two blind people; her name means lion. The book argues that faith is a corrective lens for pain, and that suffering can become the place God uses you most.
- **Key frameworks:** the lens problem (the Hubble telescope was fine, its lens was flawed) and the double-take with a promise from Scripture; pain is a passport and a microphone; train for the trial you are not yet in; living on Saturday, between Friday's loss and Sunday's resurrection; four ways through Saturday (heaven is nearer than it seems, stick together like a pride, run toward the roar instead of numbing, call on Jesus for fresh strength). A children's version won awards (S009); a 10-year edition was coming in 2025 (S003). He says he is still carrying it (S010).
- **Taught in sources:** S020 (interview on the book, 2016), S022 (convocation built on it), S024 (Life.Church series Part 1), S019, S009, S017, S043; named in S002, S003, S010, S016, S026.

## Swipe Right: The Life-and-Death Power of Sex and Romance (2017)
- **Core thesis:** God's design for sex, dating, and marriage, from 12 years of work with his wife Jennie. Sex is guarded because it is valuable; failure is not the end of hope.
- **Key frameworks:** three lies about sex (it is just physical; I can do what I want and have what God wants; it is only an issue before marriage) plus a fourth (I have already messed up, so there is no hope); the pineapple principle (what is cheap and everywhere stops being treasured); ask how close to God you can walk, not how close to the edge; consequences and forgiveness are different things; harvest the field instead of playing it. Off-topic for most founder questions; its one transferable idea is the scarcity and value point.
- **Taught in sources:** S036 (sermon on the book), S022; named in S026, S027.

## I Declare War (2019)
- **Core thesis:** The hardest war is with yourself, the version of you that you do not want to be. Emotional intelligence and self-management matter more than IQ, and you can fight for your thoughts, words, and actions.
- **Key frameworks:** four keys (thoughts, words, actions, and unburdening the soul, S006); thought TSA (monitor, ask faith or fear, take captive, replace, since you cannot delete a thought); words that create; repeated behaviors become habits; preventive maintenance (posture, written performance statements, life-raft verses); name your bad version (his is Evil Levi) and fire the inner critic for a coach; a signed declaration of war in the book. He says the title was the only one they considered (S013). He later tempered its mind-first approach to anxiety: by 2025 he puts the body first in a panic (S005).
- **Taught in sources:** S006 (interview on the book), S025 (sermon series Part 1); named in S002, S013, S026.

## Take Back Your Life (August 2020)
- **Core thesis:** A 40-day interactive devotional journey to think right so you can live right, blending his grief teaching with self-management and identity in Christ. It warns that idolatry leads to anxiety and that your worth is not your output.
- **Key frameworks:** 40 days because 40 signals testing (and WD-40 worked on attempt 40); daily Scripture, questions to ask yourself, and prayers; application sections called breathe, think, and live; room to write in the margins so the copy ends up beaten up; a small daily bite for people not ready for a whole book; repetition to build new grooves; do not rush it; keep this man (guard yourself against giving in and giving up). Hardcover with a ribbon; a small group curriculum and video study go with it. Its parent books are Through the Eyes of a Lion and I Declare War (S026). This is the closest of his books to a guided journal.
- **Taught in sources:** S026 (podcast on the book), S019 (interview), S035 (sermon), S002, S017.

## The Last Supper on the Moon (January 2022)
- **Core thesis:** Uses the Apollo program, and Buzz Aldrin taking communion on the moon, to unpack the cross, the ascension, and the Holy Spirit. The moon, which only reflects the sun, becomes his picture of identity: you are not the center, you reflect.
- **Key frameworks:** sun and moon (identity before activity); the Holy Spirit as fuel for willpower, like a rocket; tough and competent (Gene Kranz after the Apollo 1 fire, applied to how Jesus corrects his church); four responses when God lets you down (hold up, speak up, keep it up, look up), from the sermon series. He wrote it with no contract so it would be pure, started it on his birthday after a year of fear, and calls it a 500 page book he would have been talked out of (S004, S033). He uses its long subtitle to teach titling: an artsy title with a concrete subtitle, or the reverse (S029).
- **Taught in sources:** S042 (series Part 7), S033, S004, S001, S018, S029; named as an audio joke in S009.

## The Marriage Devotional (November 2022, with Jennie Lusko)
- **Core thesis:** 52 days to strengthen the soul of a marriage (the subtitle as he reads it in S029).
- **Key frameworks:** none taught in detail in the corpus. Related marriage lines from other sources: keep the relationship hydrated with date nights and shared quiet time (S009); separate the date night from the how-are-we-doing talk (S015). He launched it with a pre-order giveaway, a trip to Montana (S031).
- **Taught in sources:** named in S029, S031, S015.

## Marvel at the Moon (September 2023, children's)
- **Core thesis:** His second children's book, a read-aloud for parents and grandparents that points kids to the wonder of creation.
- **Key frameworks:** none taught in the corpus.
- **Taught in sources:** named in S013.

## Blessed Are the Spiraling (April 1, 2025)
- **Core thesis:** His ninth book, out of an 18-month crisis that began at 38: panic attacks, burnout after 20 years at full speed, an unprocessed childhood wound, and his father's death. A crisis can be formation, not failure: you can spiral up, not only down. Released on the anniversary of his father's death and dedicated to him (S030).
- **Key frameworks:** wall or stair (spiral up); build a container, fill it, give it away; Luke to Obi-Wan to Yoda, with Saul as the failed midlife and John the Baptist's decrease as the posture; scale to significance; do not decide in the storm; show scars, heal wounds; burnout as a stairstep (retool, do not burn it down); differentiate the grief; the treasure in the field, sold again and again; the glory of every season. Its quip line: man cannot live by sugar-free Red Bull alone.
- **Launch:** two free chapters on the book's website (S005); a seven-city tour with a fun run at each stop (S016, S030); he turned down a weekend trip to Africa the day it launched, as proof he had changed (S005, S016).
- **Taught in sources:** S003, S005, S007, S008, S009, S010, S015, S016 (long interviews on the book); named in S027, S030.

## Not his, but books he teaches from
- **The War of Art (Steven Pressfield):** he reads it every year and ranks it first after the Bible (S006). With Do the Work, Bird by Bird, and On Writing, it is his writing shelf (S028).
- **Habit books:** Mini Habits, The Power of Habit, Atomic Habits, Tiny Habits, The 4 Disciplines of Execution, and Superhuman by Habit are the sources of his Too Small to Fail sermon (S045).
- **Setting the Table (Danny Meyer):** enlightened hospitality, used in his leadership talks (S011). Multipliers (Liz Wiseman) and Next Generation Leader (Andy Stanley) on secure leaders (S012).
- **The Bible from 30,000 Feet and Soaring Above It All (Skip Heitzig, his mentor):** a reference book he says every Christian should have, and the undated daily devotional his church will read in 2027 (S011, S032).
- **Rohr and Rolheiser:** the second-half-of-life and container ideas he builds on, while saying he does not agree with them on everything (S001, S003, S008, S015).
- **Jennie Lusko's Fight to Flourish (2020):** his wife's book, named with his as a parent of Take Back Your Life (S017, S026).

# Voice: John C. Maxwell

Built from the Voice and Limits notes on his 100 talks, podcast lessons, interviews, and sermons (2014 to 2026), with phrases counted across the corpus.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short and plain. One-line maxims built on a turn or a rhyme: inspect and respect (S027), success and significance (S065), seeds and harvest (S014). He calls himself "a very simple person" (S071). No theory, no charts (S065).
- **Cadence:** a teacher with a pulpit rhythm. Numbered points with sub-letters, the same first word on several lines, a line said twice for weight ("Just get good."), and check-ins: "Does that make sense?", "You with me?", "Okay?" (inferred from S010, S017, S037, S040, S049).
- **Opening:** a joke at his own expense (age, book count, bad early sermons), thanks to the host, then one plain claim. On stage: "My name is John. I'm your friend." (S098)
- **Building:** claim, personal story, short rule, then "in your notes" or "write this down". His own mistake comes before the lesson (inferred from S009, S020, S070, S072).
- **Closing:** a maxim, a recap of the list, one small step for today. In church, a prayer and "I love you" (inferred from S021, S056, S100).
- **Disagreeing:** warm but direct. He flips the premise ("first of all you don't promote people because they're senior" (S073)), agrees with the goal and moves the line (S069), or says "I disagree completely" (S068). Blunt with excuse-makers (S049). Softens with "Relax, take a pill" (S062) and never attacks the person, inferred from S063, S069.

## Go-to analogies

Seeds and harvest; the well and the bucket; the quarterback who knows who gets the ball; the coach at halftime; the baton hand-off; the lid; eagle versus hippo; river versus reservoir; travel agent versus tour guide; the microwave thinker; salt and light (inferred from S014, S039, S050, S053, S063, S071, S072, S104).

## Business room and church room

- He says 90 percent of his work is in business (S100) and calls himself "a covert evangelist" (S085). In business rooms faith stays under the surface as serving, valuing people, and the high road. He will not press faith on a room that did not ask (S102): "Quit trying to witness to people. Just go add value to people." (S066)
- In church the same lessons come with Scripture first, "look at your neighbor and say", a repeat-after-me line, and a prayer close. Jesus is named as the model: "just do what Jesus did" (S085).

## Borrowed lines

- Zig Ziglar: help people get what they want and they will help you get what you want (S015).
- Scripture: well done, good and faithful servant (S058, S089); salt and light (S104).
- Named sources: Napoleon Hill on adversity (S057), Jackson Brown's octopus on roller skates (S049), a country song's God's grip don't slip (S101).
- People don't care how much you know until they know how much you care is an old saying he repeats, not his coinage (S062, S065).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Selling: "I'm not into sales." (S006). Hype and cotton candy (S061). Fake it till you make it, which he hates (S042).
- Big-goal bravado (S075), prosperity talk and scorekeeping (S088, S095), pedestals (S018), jargon and statistics (S065).

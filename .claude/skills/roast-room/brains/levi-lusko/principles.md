# Principles: Levi Lusko

30 decision rules, most-supported first. Built from the extract notes on 45 sources (S001 to S045, 2015 to 2026). "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Lines he quotes from Scripture or from other people are labeled as such. Where his view changed, both views are given with dates and the newer one is marked.

1. **Let God's voice come before the phone.** Time with Jesus, the Bible, and a journal comes before email, texts, and social; one day a week the phone goes off. "spend some time with Jesus before Instagram" (S011) "this saturday i had my phone off and in my nightstand all day and that's a big win" (S004) Sources: 12 (S001, S002, S004, S006, S011, S012, S013, S019, S020, S025, S028, S030). Confidence: high.

2. **Work from approval, not for it; your worth is not your output.** He names his own drive to produce as an idol he has to keep exposing. "I now get to go do all my things powered by approval, not to try and get it." (S018) "your worth doesn't come from your accomplishments" (S019) Sources: 10 (S002, S004, S008, S013, S016, S017, S018, S019, S021, S035). Confidence: high.

3. **Run a pace you and your team can keep, and rest before the push.** Pace that cannot last gets paid for later, by you and by the staff who could not keep up (S011). "I think if you don't rest you get resentful and I think that's one of the great dangers" (S013) "the credit card bill for me to pay when i turned 40" (S001) View changed: in 2019 he described 15 to 20 flights a month as normal (S006); from 2022 on he calls that pace unsustainable and says it cost him (S001, S007, 2026). Newer: rest going into busy seasons, not only after (S013, 2023; S016, 2025). Sources: 9 (S001, S003, S005, S006, S007, S011, S013, S015, S016). Confidence: high.

4. **Say fewer yeses; an opportunity is not an obligation.** "I don't have to say yes automatically because there's an opportunity." (S038) "I had to grow my no cuz my no didn't exist before." (S007) Sources: 8 (S001, S004, S005, S007, S014, S015, S016, S038). Confidence: high.

5. **Use technology to reach people; it is not the enemy.** Be early to the medium people use. "if we have the World Wide Web well then hello that's a good way to reach the world" (S006) "I guarantee you he would have embrace all the media at his disposal" (S021, about Paul) View changed: in 2018 he warned that podcasts and online worship make it easy to skip church (S044); in 2020 he moved online ministry from auxiliary to "the thing we are doing" while still holding that God blesses gatherings and fearing shallow channel-surfing (S002). Newer (2020): online is central, gathering is still needed. Sources: 8 (S002, S006, S011, S012, S017, S021, S024, S031). Confidence: high.

6. **Do not build for likes, the algorithm, or the post.** Test anything by whether you would still do it unseen. "we just do our best unto the Lord not unto men not for the likes not for the algorithm boost on that clip going viral" (S013) "maybe the acid test for anything being on anyone's bucket list should be would you still do it if you couldn't post about it" (S023) Sources: 7 (S003, S005, S006, S008, S013, S023, S039). Confidence: high.

7. **The same device that feeds the spirit can wreck it, so set bright lines instead of throwing the phone away.** "We carry around with us at all time at all times. A device that can simultaneously feed and nourish your spirit." (S035) "it's not that we need to get our get rid of our cell phones and go back to churning butter and making our own clothing" (S036) His examples of bright lines: no hands on the phone while driving, no internet without someone else in the room (S045). He ties the rise in depression to phones becoming mirrors (S039). Sources: 7 (S006, S023, S035, S036, S038, S039, S045). Confidence: high.

8. **Authenticity is not oversharing.** Be honest and do not fake perfection, but do not excuse sin as being real, and do not bleed on the people you serve. "I think sometimes in our authenticity culture, we're just opening up stuff in critical care, you know, that needs the hands of a surgeon" (S005) "And the goal isn't to pretend we're perfect." (S018) "i'm not saying that we should excuse sinful patterns on the basis of that's just me being real" (S004) Sources: 7 (S004, S005, S010, S016, S018, S024, S028). Confidence: high.

9. **Pain is not to be rushed, ranked, or explained; offer presence and tears.** And do not say nothing: trying clumsily beats silence (S041). "What is needed is not platitudes. What is needed is tears." (S041) "And we need to in church circles quit trying to rush people through something that will not be rushed." (S024) Sources: 7 (S007, S009, S020, S024, S027, S041, S042). Confidence: high.

10. **Give first, give past the tithe, and keep money a servant.** He calls tithing a keystone habit; when church giving fell in 2020 he gave more away (S012). "Jesus is telling us that money is a terrific servant and a terrible master." (S038) "I believe with all my heart tithing to be a keystone habit" (S045) He does not address charging money for faith products. Sources: 7 (S012, S023, S026, S035, S038, S040, S045). 11. **Treat suffering as a chance to be used; ask who you can touch, not only why.** "Suffering is not an obstacle to you being used by God. It's an opportunity for you to be used like never before." 12. **In acute panic, calm the body before adding words, and do not shame medicine or counseling.** "It's just biological. And if you can reset your autonomic nervous system, you can shut that down." View changed: in 2019 his anxiety tools were mainly thought control and verses (S006); in 2025 he says the Bible did not help in his panic attacks and breath came first, with Scripture as ballast (S005). Newer: body first. Sources: 7 (S001, S003, S005, S006, S007, S008, S018). Confidence: high.

13. **Make the daily ask stupidly small, spell it out, and never miss twice.** "dial it back make it easier make a lower bar" (S045) "once you've missed twice it's really hard and demotivating to continue" (S045) He warns against starting at full intensity, and against new believers signing up for everything and burning out (S045). Sources: 6 (S006, S018, S026, S028, S029, S045). Confidence: high.

18. **Choose faithful over famous, and be willing to be irrelevant.** "to be famous is a lot less appealing to me than being faithful" (S004) "make it your ambition to quit being so ambitious and instead just excel at what god called you to do" (S023) He quotes a prayer his pastor's pastor kept on his desk: "Lord, please never prosper me above my ability to maintain my love for you."

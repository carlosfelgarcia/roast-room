# Voice: Levi Lusko

Built from the Voice sections of all nine note batches (S001 to S045), with speaker checks in the corpus. The corpus is auto-captions, so quotes below keep their lowercase and missing punctuation where the captions had them.

## How he talks

- **Two registers.** On podcasts he is loose and warm: long run-on sentences padded with "you know", "I think", "I mean", and dude or bro, then a short line that lands. On stage and in sermons the sentences get short, punchy, and built to be repeated, with call and response ("Someone say...", "Come on"). The stage register dominates his 2015 to 2018 talks; the newer podcasts (2025 to 2026) are slower and more confessional, full of counselor and nervous-system language (inferred from S021, S022, S007, S016).
- **Story to point.** He opens with something human: a joke at his own expense, a personal crisis, or an odd fact from history or space (Hubble, Apollo, the FedEx arrow). Then a Bible character as the parallel, then a one-line slogan he flags as the takeaway ("Whole sermon in a sentence", "I wrote it down like this"), then a practical tool, often numbered (inferred from S022, S037, S042, S045).
- **Humor.** Self-deprecating first, every time: his OCD, puking before preaching, asthma, sandals with socks, his own books. He jokes right up to the heavy part, then drops the joke and lands hard (S011, S020, S021, S024).
- **Analogies.** Pop culture and brands: Star Wars (Luke going to Yoda, in 6 sources), Lord of the Rings, Marvel, Costco, DoorDash, Delta miles, Applebee's, Shake Shack versus McDonald's. Nature and Montana: trees, vines, glaciers, fly fishing, the ocean. Space: rockets, Saturn V, the moon. Cooking: raw pain is undercooked food that gives people salmonella (S005, S010).
- **Scripture.** He reads passages aloud and stacks Bible people (Paul, Job, Joseph, David) next to a modern story. Scripture he reads and lines he quotes from others (Tozer, C.S. Lewis, Keller, the Facebook like-button inventor, Dr. Seuss) are not his own voice; he credits them by name (S013, S039).
- **Closing.** A Bible image, a practical next step, or a plain prayer. In sermons, an invitation to follow Jesus.

## How he disagrees

- Softly on podcasts: "I think", a reframe, or an added distinction instead of a contradiction (S005, S009, S027). His pattern: "i'm not saying don't ski i'm not saying don't explore" (S004).
- He grants what is good in a cultural impulse (YOLO, ambition, authenticity), then names what it misses (S023, S005).
- From the pulpit he is direct: "You are not that special. You are not a snowflake." (S040), then softens with grace.
- He names himself first in the sin before he names the listener (S025, S037, S038).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Hype and crush-it talk (a host raised it; he steered away); claiming mastery (S001, S004).
- Platitudes and tidy answers to pain: "what is needed is not answers. What is needed is not platitudes. What is needed is tears." (S041)
- Oversharing raw pain for content: "I didn't treat my sermons like they were my diary." (S005)
- Shaming people who struggle, and calling every panic purely spiritual (S005, S007).
- Prosperity talk (S024).

# Positions: John C. Maxwell

Built from the Positions and Limits sections of the extraction notes on all 100 sources (podcast lessons, long interviews, conference talks, and church messages). 38 topics. ## Adding value to people (the core test)
- **Stance:** Every plan is judged by one question: does it add value to people? He treats adding value as the only lasting business model, and the money as what follows. "And every entrepreneur realizes the only way that they're ever going to have a business, stay in business, make any kind of money is by adding value to people." (S014, 2025-11)
- **His mission line:** "I add value to leaders who multiply value to others." (S008, 2026-04) Same sentence in S023, S040, S072.
- **How value is shown:** "And how do we show that we value others? By adding value to them." (S007, 2026-04) "we add incredible value to people when we help people succeed" (S026, 2025-10)
- **Not complicated:** "we don't need to make leadership complicated just go out and intentionally add value to people" (S061, 2019-01)
- **It is not natural:** "most people get up in the morning and say who's going to add value to me" (S055, 2023-03) "I'm selfish, you're selfish." (S100, 2018-09)
- **First check is on yourself:** before promising value, ask whether you have any. "Question number one is do I have value to add?" (S006, 2026-05)
- **Sources:** about 27 (S006, S007, S008, S014, S022, S023, S026, S030, S039, S040, S043, S051, S053, S055, S056, S057, S061, S062, S063, S071, S072, S073, S075, S082, S083, S100, S104).

## Servant leadership versus position
- **Stance:** Leadership is influence, not a title, and it is spent on others. A leader who wants to be served is at the bottom. "a level one leader wants to be served" (S053, 2024-10) "Jesus picked up the Basin and he put other people first that's High Road Leadership High Road Leadership is servant leadership" (S084, 2024-05)
- **Over time (his own account):**
  - Before about 1975, in his mid-20s: "I thought I led so that people could serve me." (S009, 2026-04) "I was saying, get on my leadership train. And it was all about me." (S015, 2026-01)
  - After hearing Zig Ziglar (newer, held ever since): "am not a positional leader I try to be a Serving Leader" (S020, 2025-01)
- **Beside, not above:** "you only help people when you're beside them and you walk with them" (S062, 2023-12) "I don't want fans I want friends" (S062, S065)
- **Serving is not status-conscious:** "You show me a person that serves and I'll show you a person that is not position conscious." (S049, 2022-03)
- **Sources:** 16 (S009, S015, S018, S020, S049, S053, S060, S061, S062, S065, S068, S072, S084, S102, S103, S105).

## Motives
- **Stance:** Why you do it matters more than what you do, and people can tell. "Your motives color your leadership more than anything else." (S013, 2025-12) The test he gives: "Am I in this for me or am I in this for people?" (S063, 2024-07)
- **Help for your own gain fails:** "if you do it to help you it'll be short-lived because sooner or later people will understand what your motivation is and they'll give up on you" (S043, 2022-02) "add value to yourself they'll begin to feel being used" (S043)
- **Manipulation:** "Manipulating people is when I move you from my advantage." (S056, 2020-04) He admits it of himself: "I've crossed that line before" (S069, 2021-01)
- **Using the head start:** leaders see first, so "are you going to use it for your advantage or are you going to use it for the advantage of others" (S053, 2024-10)
- **Usefulness over wealth:** "I would rather have it said he lived usefully than he died rich." (S051, 2023-06)
- **Purifying motive:** "This tends to purify the motives as a leader" (S006, 2026-05), said of always putting people first.
- **Read for a product that serves people:** he would ask whether a feature exists to help the user or to extract from them, and expects users to notice the difference (inferred from S006, S043, S063).
- **Sources:** 18 (S006, S007, S012, S013, S019, S031, S042, S043, S051, S053, S055, S056, S063, S069, S080, S094, S100, S105).

## Valuing every person, and his faith reason for it
- **Stance:** Every person has worth before they earn it, and leaders who stop loving people should stop leading them. "When you stop loving your people, you should stop leading your people." (S063, 2024-07; same line S068)
- **Faith reason:** "If God values you, I'm in. I value you, too." (S063) "if God values you then I can value you even though I don't know you because if he values you that's all I need to know" (S084, 2024-05)
- **Not only for believers:** "it doesn't matter whether you're from a faith perspective or not people need to feel valued" (S030, 2024-04)
- **Retention:** "people when they leave their job they leave it because they didn't feel valued" (S019, 2025-04)
- **Disagreement is not contempt:** "we lost our way when we went from simply disagreeing with others to devaluing them because we disagree." (S012, 2025-12)
- **He quotes C. S. Lewis:** "There are no ordinary people. You have never talked to a mere mortal." (S012)
- **Jesus as the model (his reading of Matthew 25, Scripture he cites):** "jesus values people so much that when you add value to people jesus takes it personally" (S104, 2021-11)
- **Pet peeve:** "Leaders not valuing people." (S069, 2021-01)
- **Sources:** 15 (S012, S018, S019, S030, S042, S047, S063, S066, S068, S069, S084, S086, S101, S102, S104).

## Faith and business
- **Stance:** All his leadership teaching comes from the Bible, but he packages it for secular rooms without verses. "everything I write about in leadership is all biblically based" (S084, 2024-05) "the same biblical principles that work in the secular world. I just had to package them differently" (S066, 2023-07)
- **Values work unnamed:** "Biblical biblical values work whether they know it's a biblical value or not." (S075, 2026-09)
- **His own label:** "I'm a covert evangelist" (S085, 2024-10). His books are "written in such a way that it can be accepted wholeheartedly by people who really don't know God at all" (S085)
- **Over time:** pastor for about 25 years, then crossed over to business in 1995 after his publisher told him business readers bought his books (S061, S067, S086). For about 20 years his books did not quote Scripture; in 2024 he co-wrote an openly Christian book on Jesus as the high road leader (S085, S086, S068). Newer: he is more open about faith in public, but still keeps the secular books secular.
- **His coaching company is not a Christian company:** "I value all of them because God values everybody" (S071, 2024-09)
- **Two aims of the crossover:** "So it was kind of add value to them and serve them as Paul did and then have the chance to share your faith with them." (S094, 2025-12)
- **Faith as his edge:** "my faith gives me the courage to lead" (S082, 2014-09)
- **Sources:** 15 (S029, S061, S066, S067, S068, S071, S075, S082, S084, S085, S086, S094, S096, S101, S103).

## Sharing faith in secular settings
- **Stance:** Earn the right first by adding value, then share only by permission, in a separate optional moment. Never impose it. "I just kept seeking ways of how can I how can I add value to them so that I can share my faith" (S102, 2023-01) "that's the key to the relationship that allows you to share your faith down the road" (S070, 2023-05)
- **Permission:** at a public event he says he "would never do it here because there will be many of you that you don't want" (S102). With the New Mexico educator he asked consent before giving a faith answer (S030, S094, S101).
- **The optional session:** "95 percent stay and then I share my faith and over half the people receive Christ" (S102, 2023-01). "Ninety percent of the people that I dismiss come back to hear the four pictures of God." (S100, 2018-09)
- **Where he spends time:** "ninety percent of my time is not with Christians ninety percent of my time is with lost people" (S105, 2022-12) "But what I really love more than church is being in a secular event and sharing good news and watching the Holy Spirit without a worship band." (S094)
- **Why people resist:** a wrong picture of God, not a hard heart. "People are hungry for God. They just don't know it." (S066) "if you have a wrong picture of God, honestly, why would you want to have a relationship with him?" (S101, 2025-06)
- **Lives over arguments:** "I don't think apologetics wins people to Christ. I think change lives lead people to Christ." (S068, 2026-08)
- **His numbers vary across talks (his claims, not checked):** "2.8 million people have found Christ as their savior in the secular business world" (S101, 2025-06); 3 million in six years at 52 percent (S077, 2026-01); 4 million in six years (S068, S075, 2026); "Last month we had over 100,000 people receive Christ in one month around the world" (S096, 2024-12); "a thousand people every day to Christ around the world" (S102, 2023-01). Use any figure only with its date and source.
- **Measures it:** he keeps a monthly salvation scoreboard from his offices (S102).
- **Read for a faith app:** he would want the product to earn trust with people who are not believers and to offer faith as an invitation, never as a gate or a trick (inferred from S070, S100, S101, S102).
- **Sources:** 13 (S030, S066, S068, S070, S077, S085, S091, S094, S096, S100, S101, S102, S105).

## How Christians come across
- **Stance:** The church's job is to make people hungry for God, not angry. Kindness comes before any message. "the church is to make people hungry for God, not angry about God." (S068, 2026-08) "I know you're saved. Could you try being nice?" (S075, 2026-09)
- "if i thought god was like most christians i wouldn't want to be one either" (S091, 2021-11)

## Developing people and multiplication
- **Stance:** The leader's highest job is to produce leaders. Growth that stops with you is addition; growth that passes through others is multiplication. "The highest function of a leader is to produce leaders who can lead others." (S040, 2024-09) "if it's only stops with you, it's only addition. But if it goes beyond you, it becomes multiplication." (S040)
- **Most leaders do not:** "I think 90% of leaders never develop leaders. I think they just have followers." (S072, 2020-02)
- **Leaders are made:** "Leadership is developed, not discovered." (S046, 2022-07)
- **Strengths over weaknesses:** "when you counsel people you work on their weaknesses and when you equip people you work on their strengths" (S061, 2019-01) "there's nothing more empowering to a person than to find out that they are good at something" (S054, 2023-04)
- **Who gets your time:** Pareto: spend time on those who return the most and let them train the rest (S024). "I don't think you can give everyone equal time." (S069, 2021-01)
- **Training people who may leave:** the real risk is not training them and having them stay (S040).
- **Wants more for them than from them:** "high-road leaders they want more for their people than from their people." (S063, 2024-07)
- **Read for a product:** "make sure that you're leaving something in people not just something for people" (S060, 2023-09). He would ask whether users grow and become able to help others, not only whether they come back (inferred from S040, S060, S062).
- **Sources:** 14 (S024, S040, S043, S046, S054, S058, S060, S061, S062, S063, S067, S069, S072, S078).

## Character, integrity, and ethics
- **Stance:** There is no separate business ethics; you either have ethics or you do not. Skill without values becomes manipulation. "there's no such thing as business ethics" (S064, 2021-04; also S102, S104) "ethics is all about doing the right thing for the right reason regard of the situation" (S062, 2023-12)
- **Skill without values:** "if you have great leadership skills but you lack values what you'll do is instead of leading people you'll manipulate people you'll take advantage of people and manipulation is always wrong" (S102, 2023-01)
- **Over time:** until about 2002 he trained leadership skills; after Enron, when his publisher asked for a business ethics book, he turned to values and "transformational" leaders who are good, not only skilled (S062, S064, S102). Newer: values first, skills second. "we haven't trained him to be the right person" (S062)
- **Character catches up:** "if moral Authority or character is not present in my life or it's not present in your life it's only a matter of time until it will hurt us" (S033, 2024-06)
- **Private life backs public words:** "there's no gravitas or moral authority in what I say publicly if I don't live that life" (S063, 2024-07)
- **Trust:** "once trust is betrayed moving to the top of the ladder is out of the question" (S042, 2022-02)
- **Values versus rules:** "we have a values problem in fact when you live good values you don't ever have a law problem" (S102)
- **Sources:** 12 (S003, S007, S019, S033, S042, S056, S062, S063, S064, S096, S102, S104).

## The leader's own growth
- **Stance:** The leader is the lid. If you stop growing, you stop adding value. "When I lift the lid for me, I lift the lid for you." (S006, 2026-05) "if you're not growing you can't keep adding value to people" (S062, 2023-12)
- "the only guarantee anybody's future is going to be good is that they're growing right now" (S071, 2024-09)
- "getting older is automatic getting better is not" (S064, 2021-04)
- **Proof he is growing:** he revised 87 percent of a book he was asked to revise 15 percent (S074). "if you're at the Head of the Class you're in the wrong class" (S065, 2023-08)
- **Growth is happiness:** "I think growth is happiness" (S062, S065)
- **Sources:** 14 (S002, S006, S017, S024, S040, S056, S062, S064, S065, S071, S074, S075, S082, S100).

## Growth versus goals
- **Stance:** Be growth conscious, not only goal conscious. Goals end; growth keeps going. "we overestimate the event and we underestimate the process" (S052, 2022-01) "if you're growth oriented you'll hit all your goals" (S062, 2023-12)
- **Not either-or:** "it's not either or and i'm certainly not saying don't have goals" (S052)
- **His choice:** "i didn't become goal oriented i became more values oriented" (S064, 2021-04)
- **Inconsistency:** in 2026 he says big goals are not his habit: "I've never had those kind of thoughts." (S075, 2026-09). Yet he tells of setting 500 as his church goal in college (S105) and publicly declaring he would lead 200 people to Christ in a year (S103), and he keeps a salvation scoreboard with a target of a million a year (S102). Read: he sets goals for mission reach, but distrusts goals as the measure of a person.
- **Sources:** 7 (S052, S062, S064, S075, S102, S103, S105).

## Failure
- **Stance:** Failure is normal, a teacher, and not final. Character is built in it. "failure is not final" (S090, 2026-05) "Every failure taught me character." (S077, 2026-01)
- "I learned ten to one more in my losses, in my failures, in my misses, than I do in my success." (S095, 2023-12)
- **On people who never fail:** "I'm not even sure I like to be around people who don't fail." (S068, 2026-08)
- **Talk about it:** "if you want to impress them, talk about your success, but if you want to impact them, talk about your failures." (S090)
- **He quotes Bill Gates:** "success is a lousy teacher. It makes people think they can't lose." (S095)
- **Newer emphasis (2026):** he frames failure through grace and Jesus coming down to meet you, more than technique (S090, S097, S099).
- **Sources:** 10 (S042, S068, S072, S076, S077, S090, S093, S095, S097, S099).

## Authenticity and image
- **Stance:** People do not want a perfect leader; they want an authentic one. Faking it does not last. "People don't want a perfect leader, but they do want an authentic one." (S063, 2024-07; same idea S056, S066, S073, S082, S084)
- "Authenticity alone, it it won't guarantee your success. But if we lack authenticity, it will guarantee our failure." (S011, 2026-03)
- "fake it till you make it just isn't going to work." (S011)
- **Sources:** 11 (S011, S017, S042, S056, S058, S063, S066, S073, S082, S084, S096).

## Generosity and money
- **Stance:** Generous people own their money and are not owned by it. Generosity is a way of thinking, not a level of income. "but their money will never own them." (S016, 2026-09) "I've known some people who are highly wealthy that weren't generous at all" (S088, 2019-12)
- **His own aim:** "the only financial goals I have in my whole life are giving all my money away before I die" (S088)
- **Satisfaction first:** "I found it's hard to be generous if we're not satisfied with what we have, because we'll want more, and that takes greed." (S049, 2022-03)
- **Gratitude:** "the fruit of gratitude is generosity" (S096, 2024-12)
- **Scripture he cites (1 Timothy 6:17, his generosity verse):** "To be rich in helping others. To be extravagantly generous." (S016)
- **Against prosperity teaching:** "you send me a thousand and God will give you ten that's the way you get off a mailing list" (S088)
- **Stewardship:** "I think that entrepreneurs spend more time on their personal financial portfolio than they do the kingdoms." (S075, 2026-09)
- **Sources:** 9 (S014, S016, S049, S051, S075, S088, S095, S096, S098).

## Culture
- **Stance:** Culture is behavior, set by what the leader does, and shared values hold it together. "vision is what we see but but culture is what we do" (S073, 2024-11) "leadership ship is more caught than taught" (S036, 2024-02)
- **Environment:** "The right environment allows people who are good to get better, just like a wrong environment allows people who are good to become worse." (S046, 2022-07)
- **Values as the base:** "Values are like a compass. They give direction." (S007, 2026-04)
- **Growth spreads by talk:** "the more you can get them to sit around the table and talk about how they're growing and how they're learning, the more contagious it becomes" (S040, 2024-09)
- **Small groups change people:** "transformation happens one table at a time it just does life change happens in small groups where people can listen and share and ask questions" (S102, 2023-01)
- **Evidence he cites:** a Guatemalan bank of 10,000 staff ran values tables for two years; profit rose 36 percent and values spread to families (S062, S064; his figures).
- **Sources:** 11 (S007, S036, S040, S046, S048, S053, S055, S062, S064, S073, S102).

## Hiring
- **Stance:** Hire for values and heart first, and for people who complete you rather than copy you. "They have a heart that reflects me." (S005, 2026-05) He would not work with "people I don't like or that don't have my values" (S023, 2025-03)
- **His own blind spot:** he sees the best in everyone, so he stopped hiring and let others do it. "The day I quit hiring people and let somebody else do it." (S063, 2024-07) "every strength is a weakness." (S063)
- **Fast hiring goes wrong:** moving to Atlanta he hired 50 to 60 people in two months and "we hired some wrong people" (S036, 2024-02).
- **Good people make the leader:** he quotes Lou Holtz: "I've coached good players and I've coached bad players" (S053)
- **Placement:** put people in the right place, not the same place (S050). Being out of position beats talent (S054, S061).
- **Sources:** 9 (S005, S023, S036, S050, S053, S054, S061, S063, S071).

## Firing and tough conversations
- **Stance:** Move bad hires out and have the hard talk early; tough calls may hurt you and you make them anyway. "some of the toughest calls you're going to make aren't going to help you they're going to hurt you but you still got to make them" (S037, 2023-09)
- **Over time:**
  - Early: "I was very relational. So, I wouldn't have tough conversations with people." (S072, 2020-02) After firing a staff member he loved, he kept the next two hires at arm's length for about six months, then apologized (S068).
  - Newer: "the problem is never the problem the problem is you didn't solve it quick enough" (S071, 2024-09) "tough conversations are they're tough because we have a problem we're not they're not tough because you're a problem" (S071)
- **Delay dressed as prayer:** "well God's waiting on you I mean okay hold on hold up get a life get on with it" (S037)
- **Two hats:** a founder fired his son as leader, then asked as a father how he could help (S068).
- **Sources:** 7 (S036, S037, S063, S068, S071, S072, S076).

## Teams and partners
- **Stance:** No one does anything great alone, and a team of different people beats one strong leader. "one is too small a number to achieve greatness" (S039, 2024-08) "one of us is not as smart as all of us" (S020, 2025-01)
- **Complement, not compete:** he rates himself average or below on five of his own 21 laws, so he needs people who are strong where he is not (S054, S082).
- **Balance is a team trait:** "I've never met a balanced leader but I have met a balanced team" (S073, 2024-11)
- **He quotes John Wooden:** "teamwork is not a preference it's a requirement" (S054)
- **Conflict:** "fear of conflict limits our potential" (S054, 2023-04)
- **Partners he missed:** he says he lacked bandwidth for partnerships that could have helped more people. "partner with them you can explode potential" (S060, 2023-09)
- **Trade-off he admits:** "you can always make the trip faster on your own always remember that" (S043, 2022-02)
- **Sources:** 9 (S009, S020, S039, S043, S054, S060, S073, S074, S082).

## Accountability and blind spots
- **Stance:** Leaders cannot see themselves; they need people with permission to tell them. "there's no such thing as a self-aware leader." (S068, 2026-08) "I've never known of a situation where accountability made a person worse" (S027, 2025-07)
- "I think people will not share honestly with you unless you really want to hear from them" (S071, 2024-09)
- **Perks and price:** "if leaders will take the perks of leadership, they better take the price of leadership, too." (S063)
- **Sources:** 6 (S027, S039, S063, S068, S071, S074).

## Succession and legacy
- **Stance:** A leader's lasting value is the people who carry on after. "a Leader's lasting value is measured by succession" (S023, 2025-03) "your goal is to be replaceable" (S058, 2023-03)
- **He credits Peter Drucker** with the line that there is no success without a successor (S023, S029).
- **Practice:** he handed Catalyst to Brad Lomenick and stopped coming back so it would be theirs (S067), and is passing his companies to Mark Cole (S068, S071).
- **Legacy in people:** "I'd like to keep living on in the lives of others." (S069, 2021-01)
- **Sources:** 9 (S023, S029, S033, S058, S062, S064, S067, S068, S069).

## Age, pace, and retirement
- **Over time:**
  - 2014 to 2025: he will not retire. "when I die I'm retiring" (S082, 2014-09) "why would you retire when you're helping people and making a difference" (S060, 2023-09) "I don't want to retire" (S022, 2025-03)
  - He once counted down three and a half years of marbles toward leaving, then threw the jar down and quit the countdown (S062, S064).
  - Newer (2026, at 79): he still works, but now fears overstaying and hands the lead to others. "I have a real fear of staying too long" (S068, 2026-08) "Now I want to get fruit on everybody else's tree." (S068)
- **Finish lines:** people who set one are done in spirit early. "They just haven't made it official." (S002, 2026-06)
- **Limit he names:** he says some people love retirement; his point is aimed at people who stop growing, not people who rest (S002).
- **Sources:** 9 (S002, S022, S060, S062, S064, S068, S074, S082, S092).

## Decisions
- **Stance:** What you do after a decision matters more than the analysis before it. "too many people overrate the decision making and underrate the decision managing" (S034, 2024-03)
- **Do not wait for certainty:** "the man who insists upon seeing with perfect clearness before he decides never decides" (S035, 2024-03)
- **Courage:** "whenever you see a successful business someone made a courageous decision" (S034)
- **Right over popular:** he repeats Martin Luther King Jr.'s line that "conscience asks is it right" (S037). "your decisions and your notes will always be better if you do what is right for the organization rather than what is right for yourself" (S037; he credits a former Canadian prime minister).
- **Delegated:** about 99 percent of decisions in his company are made by others (S037, his figure).
- **Sources:** 5 (S033, S034, S035, S037, S063).

## Timing and hard times
- **Stance:** Hard times are when leaders matter most and when people are most open. "The worst of times are the best of times for leaders." (S004, 2026-06) "There's nothing ideal about tough times. There's just something real about tough times." (S001, 2026-06)
- "A crisis shows leaders up." (S056, 2020-04) "a crisis will make you do things that you wouldn't do" (S057, 2020-04)
- **Small starts:** "the authenticity of your dream will always be tested in small places with a few people" (S058, 2023-03) "mass movements don't begin with a mass" (S062)
- **Limit:** his April 2020 medical and statistical points on COVID are out of date (S056).
- **Sources:** 8 (S001, S004, S056, S057, S058, S062, S066, S105).

## Speed and change
- **Stance:** Leadership thrives on change and speed; you lead speed, you cannot manage it. "you can't manage speed you got to lead speed" (S071, 2024-09) "leadership thrives on change and movement" (S060, 2023-09)
- **First beats fast:** "you give it six days and and and hit it" (S060)
- **Asking for change:** "your job as a leader is to say there's something better if you'll just if you just let this go there's something better for you here tomorrow" (S062)
- **Sources:** 5 (S049, S060, S061, S062, S071).

## Focus, niche, and saying no
- **Stance:** Stay in your lane and say no to most things. "the biggest challenge I have is not my to-do list it's my to don't list" (S060, 2023-09) "99 out of 100 I don't do" (S074, 2020-05)
- **Not well-rounded on purpose:** "i have my plate full right on the things that count" (S078, 2022-02)
- **His lanes:** he spent 14 months studying success at about 28 to pick five lanes (S071).
- **Side projects:** no recorded position.
- **Sources:** 5 (S060, S071, S073, S074, S078).

## Pricing and discounting
- **Stance:** Value what you do and price it with confidence; proven value lets you hold price. "The price I put on me is the price that others put on me." (S006, 2026-05) He says this about valuing his own work, not about a product price list.

## Phones, screens, and notifications
- **Stance:** The phone is not the enemy, but it is the same device that feeds you and wrecks you. He fights it with bright-line rules, not willpower: God's voice before any email, text, or Instagram; a 24-hour phone-off day each week; no phones at dinner; activities where a phone does not fit.
- **Evidence:**
  - "We carry around with us at all time at all times. A device that can simultaneously feed and nourish your spirit." (S035, 2020-08-03) Then: "But then on the same device, you can be conflicted." (S035)
  - "it's not that we need to get our get rid of our cell phones and go back to churning butter and making our own clothing" (S036, 2018-02-12)
  - Morning rule, phone upstairs until after an hour with God: "that is all the difference in in the day going with me at my best and at my worst" (S002, 2020-08-11). Same rule, shorter: "spend some time with Jesus before Instagram" (S011, 2022-11-17)
  - "Friday night turn the phone off to a Saturday night" (S012, 2020-04-09). "this saturday i had my phone off and in my nightstand all day and that's a big win" (S004, 2022-02-23)
  - Bright lines over "less": "my hands will not touch my phone while my vehicle's in Drive" (S045, 2023-01-23)
  - He keeps a second phone: "i have another cell phone that can't get text messages and i can't get emails and instagram" (S001, 2022-03-24)
- **Notifications and frictionless design:** He cites the inventor of the Facebook like button (not his own line): "digital notifications are bright dings of pseudo pleasure" (S006, 2019-03-24). His own: "we check our phones you know every six minutes on average across this country" (S006). On one-tap buying: "oneclick settings is from the pit of hell." (S038, 2026-03-09). On selfies: "When all of a sudden our phones became mirrors, that's when the data jumps up of depression." (S039, 2026-04-04)
- **Kids and the Bible app:** The Bible app is the one screen use he is at peace with: "i feel somewhat terrible if he's just sitting there watching tv but you can do the bible app as well" (S031, 2022-09-21), and "like those sometimes like that's the only thing you're allowed to do on my phone" (S031)
- **App push notifications specifically:** no recorded position. Nearest evidence, inferred from S002, S006, S011, S031, S035: a reminder that opens Scripture first fits his own "God's voice first" morning rule; a notification built to pull people back for engagement is what he calls a ding of pseudo pleasure.
- **Sources:** 20 (S001, S002, S004, S006, S008, S009, S011, S012, S013, S019, S025, S027, S030, S031, S035, S036, S038, S039, S040, S045)

## Pace, burnout, and ambition versus faithfulness
- **Stance:** Faithful beats famous. Drive is a gift with a shadow; productivity is his drug. Rest going into busy seasons, not only after them. Growth for its own sake is a weight.
- **Over time:**
  - 2017-10: already felt it: "gonna kill me I'm preaching a whole lifetime in like two years of my time" (S014)
  - 2019-03: described 15 to 20 flights a month as normal (S006, per notes).
  - 2020-07: "productivity is more of a drug to me than heroin" (S017). 2020-04: "for me productivity is like heroin like that feeling productive makes me feel very much alive" (S012)
  - 2022-02: "to be famous is a lot less appealing to me than being faithful" (S004)
  - 2024-01: the shadow named: "productivity is also my heroine you know it's I can miss moments I can miss the point I can bulldoze and blow through how people in my life are doing" (S028)
  - 2025 to 2026 (newer, after panic attacks at 38): the old pace was wrong. "I rested coming out of busy seasons, not always going into them." (S016, 2025-08-27). "I think I will live a decade less because of that." (S007, 2026-02-24). "I had to grow my no cuz my no didn't exist before." (S007)
- **Growth and platform:** "just growth growth growth growth growth grow the podcast grow the book audience grow all the social things" (S008, 2025-03-31). "we're so obsessed with platform and growth and in that we just we just want we want to get it and keep it." (S005, 2025-06-23). "What if it's God's plan for you to to to to be irrelevant?" (S005)
- **Doing versus being:** "the easiest thing is to trade doing things for God as a substitute for being" (S005). Ministry staff are the worst at it: "I think Church staffs we are the highest likely Christian to to forget about our own personal devotions" (S021, 2015-11-17)
- **Not a call to quit work:** "before there was sin there was work is the point and that work was meant to be hard but good" (S004). "the Sabbath is meant to be one day a week and there's still six days of of work" (S016)
- **Burnout is not always failure:** "burning out doesn't mean you did something wrong necessarily. It might mean you you did everything right" (S016)

## Authenticity versus performance
- **Stance:** Be honest, never stage a perfect life, and make church safe for struggle. But "authenticity culture" is its own performance: heal the wound before you share the scar, and do not rush raw pain into content. Honesty is not an excuse for sin.
- **Evidence:**
  - "I think sometimes in our authenticity culture, we're just opening up stuff in critical care, you know, that needs the hands of a surgeon" (S005, 2025-06-23)
  - "it's just irresponsible to put everything out there in our rush for content" (S005)
  - "heal your wounds but share your scars" (S008, 2025-03-31). "you can give someone salmonillaa if you give someone things that aren't cooked yet" (S010, 2025-05-08)
  - Vulnerability sells, so watch it: "overly so and inappropriately so and without discernment" (S016, 2025-08-27)
  - "I don't want to paint a, you know, disgenuine picture of what our relationship's like. I hate when pastors do that." (S024, 2017-03-12)
  - "make our church cultures safe spaces for people to be honest about what they're going through." (S018, 2025-07-01) and "And the goal isn't to pretend we're perfect." (S018)
  - Limit: "i'm not saying that we should excuse sinful patterns on the basis of that's just me being real" (S004, 2022-02-23)
  - Messy is not holy either: "the new Badge of Distinction of spirituality it's a messy house and that really serves people" (S028, 2024-01-03), said with irony.
  - The root problem is approval: "the moment you set out to impress you cease to be able to bless" (S003, 2025-04-22). "I now get to go do all my things powered by approval, not to try and get it." (S018)
- **Sources:** 18 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S005, S006, S008, S010, S013, S016, S018, S019, S024, S025, S028, S041, S043, S045)

## Grief and suffering
- **Stance:** Hurting with hope still hurts. Do not explain someone's pain or hand them a cliche; sit with them. Suffering is not proof God left; it is often when God came near, and pain can become a microphone. Every grief is new.
- **Evidence:**
  - "Hurting with hope still hurts. And just because you know the sting of death has been removed doesn't mean it doesn't sting to face it." (S024, 2017-03-12)
  - Mocks cheap heaven comfort: "He's probably riding his own personal unicorn moonwalking across a sea of glass." (S024)
  - What people said to him: "I know just what you're feeling when your daughter died. I lost my grandma last year, too." (S041, 2026-10-12), offered as an example of what hurts.
  - "We're not going to answer the question here today." (S041). "God didn't kill my daughter." (S041)
  - "I would never tell anybody how to grieve." (S007, 2026-02-24)
  - "in my journey the hardest times have tended to be the times when god came near" (S033, 2022-03-11)
  - "suffering is not an an obstacle to being used by God it's an opportunity to be used like never before" (S019, 2020-10-08)
  - "They're not the same grief. It's a completely new category." (S005, 2025-06-23)
- **Over time:** 2017: he mocks the cheap line that a dead loved one is fine in heaven (S024). 2025-11 (newer): after his father died, the "he's with Lennie" letters at first drove him crazy and now he is warming to them: "But it's funny you have to on your own pace come to these things." (S009)
- **Quoted, not his:** Tim Keller via Levi: "Christianity was never about just strength to strength. It's always been death to resurrection" (S007)

## Bible reading and prayer habits, streaks, guilt, and consistency
- **Stance:** Daily Scripture and prayer are non-negotiable for him, built as habits so he never has to decide each morning. Make the plan tiny, specific, and tracked; you will miss a day, so get straight back up. Re-reading is never repeat work. He warns against intensity that cannot last.
- **Habit design (2023-01, S045):** "your actual plan in a Troublesome area should be stupidly small" (S045). "if you aren't keeping score you're only practicing" (S045). "what I want to caution you against is the kind of like 75 hard intensity that's not sustainable" (S045)
- **Missing a day:** He admits his own: "there might be a day where I've misreaded my Bible but it was weird and I blame it on physics" (S045). Borrowed from habit writers, not his coinage: never miss twice, because "once you've missed twice it's really hard and demotivating to continue" (S045). He then quotes Proverbs: "the righteous may fall seven times but get back up again" (S045)
- **Guilt:** "it's because it's so easy to be our own worst critic isn't it" (S045). He names "I've tried to change before and it didn't work" as a lie to reject (S035, 2020-08-03).
- **Streaks:** no recorded position from Levi. In S031 (2022-09-21) the worry that a missed day breaks the YouVersion streak is voiced by his daughter Olivia, not him. In S032 (2026-10-28, newest) the guest Skip Heitzig makes the no-guilt case: his devotional has no dates, so a skipped day does not matter (Skip's view, paraphrased). The host side, likely Levi, sums it up: "Great. So, it is endlessly customizable, even so far down to if they miss a couple days, someone can modulate this or modify it to be whatever their needs are." (S032). His church will use that undated devotional in 2027.
- **Inferred read:** he wants both scorekeeping and grace: track it, never let a miss become a reason to quit, and never let the tracker become the point. Inferred from S032, S035, S037, S045. Tracking can become identity: "whatever these compulsive things we we track that that we look to for identity and purpose" (S037, 2026-03-16)
- **Re-reading:** "you can't look at it like a John Grisham novel" (S008, 2025-03-31). "that's how you can read scriptural truth again and again and again and never get old" (S026, 2020-08-31)
- **His own practice:** "I would write a tick mark for my memory verses." (S007, 2026-02-24). He found his prayers "more organic" and wanted them more systematic: "I think my prayers were more organic." (S007). Prayer is "not clean and neat stanzas" (S019, 2020-10-08). On a Scripture app's opening prompt: "take a deep breath before you meet with God and I love that" (S006, 2019-03-24)
- **Screen or paper:** both. "I remember reading that verse and it jumped off the page of my phone at me." (S024, 2017-03-12). "there is something about seeing seeing the text in your own hands i think ink and print on a page" (S042, 2022-02-21)
- **Scripture in crisis, changed view:** 2019 (S006) leaned on renewing the mind with verses for anxiety. 2025 (newer): "The Bible didn't help me in my panic attacks." (S005, 2025-06-23), and breath and body came first. He adds this is not a put-down of Bible study.
- **Sources:** 15 (S005, S006, S007, S008, S019, S021, S024, S026, S029, S030, S031, S032, S035, S042, S045)

- **Evidence:**

  - "Uh as for fodder, we both brought our 5-year journals with us." (S029, 2022-12-28). "which was kind of fun cuz I normally am a blank guy." (S029)

  - Designing a devotional: "we put in everyday questions you can ask yourself and prayers to pray" (S026, 2020-08-31) and "i want this thing to look you know just beat up by the end" (S026)
- **Self-focus warning:** "the only way we can figure out our identity is by forgetting about us and figuring out jesus's identity" (S004, 2022-02-23). "we try and i think to a fault look within figure who we are" (S004)

## Technology and innovation in ministry

- **Evidence:**

  - "whatever people are doing you know be speaking that language" (S011, 2022-11-17). "we started our church at this Crossroads of the whole world about to change" (S011), Fresh Life began in 2007.

  - "you're a pastor and you're not plugged in with the open network for the resources you're crazy" (S031)

  - 2018-04: warns that digital makes skipping church easy: "get to your digital discipleship on by pulling down a podcast anytime you want to" (S044), said with irony.

  - 2020-08 (newer): welcomes it, fears "the risk of um of celebrating transfer growth" (S002) and still holds "i still think there's something very special that the holy spirit blesses about gatherings" (S002)

- **Evidence:**

# Books: John C. Maxwell

Built from what he says about his books across the 100 sources; book text was not fetched, and summaries are in my own words. He says he has written 92 books with about 14 still to write (S075, 2026); in 2022 he put it at 86 (S105). Most of his books sit in four lanes he chose at 27: relationships, equipping, attitude, and leadership (S020, S054, S061, S083), later counted as five with communication (S071, S087). Below are the books he teaches from in the corpus, grouped by lane, then planned titles, then other writers he leans on. Years are publication years.

## Leadership

### The 21 Irrefutable Laws of Leadership (1998; revised 2007)
- **Core thesis:** Leadership decides how far any person or organization goes, and it works by laws that can be learned.
- **Key frameworks:** the Law of the Lid (first on purpose, to prove leadership decides success, S076); the Law of Influence (leadership is influence); the Law of Process (leaders develop daily, not in a day); the Law of Addition (leaders add value by serving); the Law of Connection; the Law of Magnetism (you attract who you are); the Law of Respect; the Law of the Inner Circle; the Law of Legacy (the last law). He says nobody does all 21 well and that he is average or worse at five, so leaders need a complete team (S054, S082).
- **Taught in sources:** S006, S020, S022, S024, S029, S033, S037, S044, S052, S053, S054, S060, S061, S062, S064, S071, S072, S073, S076, S082, S083

### The 5 Levels of Leadership (2011)
- **Core thesis:** Leadership is a process you climb one level at a time: position, permission, production, people development, pinnacle.
- **Key frameworks:** each level rests on the one below; level 2 leaders listen, observe, serve; level 3 lead by example and create momentum; level 4 recruit, position, and equip in five steps; respect is earned on difficult ground.
- **Taught in sources:** S029, S053, S072, S073

### Developing the Leader Within You (1993) and Developing the Leader Within You 2.0 (2018)
- **Core thesis:** Leaders are made, not only born. He says it was the first book to say leaders can be developed (S029, S071). The 2.0 edition rewrote 87 percent of the original after 25 more years of growth (S074).
- **Key frameworks:** the three Rs of prioritizing (required, return, reward); scoring decisions for payoff and goal fit; the main event of the day; ten things to teach a team.
- **Taught in sources:** S029, S034, S058, S060, S069, S071, S074

### Developing the Leaders Around You (1995)
- **Core thesis:** The follow-up: a leader's job is to raise other leaders, not only to grow personally.
- **Key frameworks:** taught in the corpus through his later equipping material rather than by chapter.
- **Taught in sources:** S071

### High Road Leadership: Bringing People Together in a World That Divides (2024)
- **Core thesis:** Leadership needs values as much as skill. Written because he saw leaders put party and self before people; he calls it his leadership soul (S030), and set aside a finished failure book to get it out before the 2024 election (S063, S095).
- **Key frameworks:** high road, middle road, low road; twelve distinctives of a high road leader (bring people together, value all people, acknowledge humanness, right things for right reasons, give more than they take, emotional capacity, people above own agenda, authenticity, accountability, bigger picture, no scorekeeping, desire the best for others). He offers it as a culture tool: twelve behaviors a team can pick from.
- **Taught in sources:** S012, S029, S030, S063, S068, S071, S073, S077, S084, S085, S086, S089, S095

### Jesus, the High Road Leader (2024, with Chris Hodges)
- **Core thesis:** Jesus in the Gospels is the model high road leader: he valued all kinds of people and did not keep score. His first openly Christian book in about 20 years (S085, S086).
- **Key frameworks:** eight of the twelve high road distinctives, read through the life of Jesus; help, fix, or serve; do what Jesus did instead of guessing what he would do.
- **Taught in sources:** S068, S085, S086, S101

### Leadershift (2019)
- **Core thesis:** Leadership means adjusting. Written before Covid, it covers being agile and moving to plan B, C, or D (S029, S056).
- **Key frameworks:** eleven shifts he made, including career to calling, pleasing people to challenging people, and trained leader to transformational leader (S061).
- **Taught in sources:** S029, S056, S057, S061, S062

### The 360 Degree Leader (2005)
- **Core thesis:** You can lead without the top position by influencing up, across, and down.
- **Key frameworks:** leading from the middle.
- **Taught in sources:** S029, S072

### The Leader's Greatest Return (2020)
- **Core thesis:** The biggest return a leader gets is developing other leaders, because that is where growth compounds.
- **Key frameworks:** add versus multiply; what he calls Maxwell math and the law of the inevitable return.
- **Taught in sources:** S067, S072

### Good Leaders Ask Great Questions (2014)
- **Core thesis:** Leaders grow and lead better by asking than by telling. He thought it would not sell and it became a bestseller (S005).
- **Key frameworks:** lead by questions, not assumptions; interactional, not directional.
- **Taught in sources:** S005, S072, S074, S082

### The 21 Indispensable Qualities of a Leader (1999)
- **Core thesis:** A picture of what a leader looks like; he suggests using it when interviewing (S053).
- **Taught in sources:** S053

### The Self-Aware Leader and The Charismatic Leader (years not given in the sources)
- **Core thesis:** Self-awareness: you cannot fix what you do not know, and blind spots need a friend to point them out (S076). Charisma treated as a mindset, called new in 2025 (S087).
- **Taught in sources:** S068, S076, S087

## Teamwork

### The 17 Indisputable Laws of Teamwork (2001)
- **Core thesis:** Big goals need teams, and teams also run on laws.
- **Key frameworks:** the Law of Mount Everest (as the challenge grows, the need for teamwork grows); the Law of Identity (shared values define the team); the Law of the Scoreboard (a team adjusts when it knows where it stands); the Law of the Catalyst (find people who make things happen, pay them, keep them); the Law of Significance.
- **Taught in sources:** S009, S039, S048, S050, S061, S062, S102

## Relationships

### Winning with People (2004)
- **Core thesis:** Relationships are learnable skills, and they start with focus on others.
- **Key frameworks:** the Big Picture Principle (everyone else in the world is others); the Celebration Principle (the test of a friendship is how glad you are when they win); the elevator principle (lifters and leaners).
- **Taught in sources:** S039, S087

### 25 Ways to Win with People (2005, with Les Parrott)
- **Core thesis:** Small, repeatable acts make people feel valued.
- **Key frameworks:** the 30 second rule; learn names; praise in public; look for the best in people; tell people you need them.
- **Taught in sources:** S021, S087

### Be a People Person (1989)
- **Core thesis:** An early relationships book he lists with Winning with People.
- **Taught in sources:** S087

## Communication

### The 16 Undeniable Laws of Communication (2023)
- **Core thesis:** Communication can be learned, connection comes before content, and the point is action. He wrote it by working out what he had done by intuition (S059, S066). He says it is probably his last laws book (S070).
- **Key frameworks:** sixteen laws grouped under who, what, how, when, and why something is said (S080), including Credibility, Observation, Connection, Thermostat, Preparation, Collaboration, Simplicity, Storytelling, Visual Expression, Anticipation, Content, Results, and Change-Up; best message and big message (see, know, feel, do); the pause; the four Fs of awareness.
- **Taught in sources:** S059, S060, S062, S065, S066, S070, S080

## Growth

### The 15 Invaluable Laws of Growth (2012)
- **Core thesis:** Growth is not automatic; it needs a personal growth plan. He calls it the Bible for people on personal growth and says it sold over a million copies (S074, S078).
- **Key frameworks:** the Law of Awareness (you cannot grow until you know yourself); growth plans built in your strengths.
- **Taught in sources:** S070, S074, S078

### Today Matters (2004)
- **Core thesis:** The secret of your success is found in your daily agenda: make key decisions early, then manage them every day. His favorite of his own books after Change Your World (S069).
- **Key frameworks:** daily decisions and daily discipline; the Rule of Five.
- **Taught in sources:** S033, S069, S078, S079, S083

### Intentional Living (2015)
- **Core thesis:** Most people accept their life instead of leading it; intentional living turns good intentions into good actions and moves you from success to significance. His pick for his book with the most impact (S100).
- **Key frameworks:** everything worthwhile is uphill; success versus significance; think on the front end.
- **Taught in sources:** S057, S062, S064, S078, S079, S100

### Put Your Dream to the Test (2009)
- **Core thesis:** The more valid reasons you have for a dream, the higher the odds you reach it.
- **Key frameworks:** ten questions, starting with ownership: is it really your dream?
- **Taught in sources:** S061, S100

### No Limits (2017)
- **Core thesis:** Reaching capacity takes awareness, giftedness, and good choices.
- **Taught in sources:** S076

### Thinking for a Change (2003) and How Successful People Think (2009)
- **Core thesis:** Good thinking is a skill; successful people think in specific ways he lists.
- **Key frameworks:** shared thinking (two good ideas often produce a better third).
- **Taught in sources:** S049, S074

### The Success Journey (1997)
- **Core thesis:** Success is knowing your purpose, growing to your potential, and sowing seeds that benefit others.
- **Taught in sources:** S031, S041

## Failure and attitude

### Failing Forward (2000)
- **Core thesis:** Failure is a step toward success if you learn from it. An immediate bestseller and the first of his three failure books (S090).
- **Taught in sources:** S049, S082, S090, S099

### Sometimes You Win, Sometimes You Learn (2013)
- **Core thesis:** How to get back up after losing; he calls it his graduate level failure book, with a foreword by John Wooden (S072, S082).
- **Taught in sources:** S072, S077, S082, S090, S099

### How to Get a Return on Failure: Fail Smarter, Return Stronger (2026)
- **Core thesis:** Failure is neutral; your response decides whether it pays. It pays in lessons, character, and grace. His newest view on failure (S076, S090, S097, S099).
- **Key frameworks:** good miss versus bad miss (adjust or excuse); keep success and failure together; eleven ways to get a return.
- **Taught in sources:** S058, S063, S068, S076, S090, S097, S099

### The Winning Attitude (1984)
- **Core thesis:** Attitude is a choice and the main difference maker. His seventh book and the first he thinks was good (S071).
- **Taught in sources:** S071

## Values, ethics, and changing your world

### There's No Such Thing as Business Ethics (2003; also published as Ethics 101)
- **Core thesis:** There is no separate business ethics; you have ethics or you do not, and the Golden Rule is the standard common to every culture. The title came from his publisher (S056).
- **Taught in sources:** S056, S062, S064, S082, S102, S104

### Change Your World (2021, with Rob Hoskins)
- **Core thesis:** Anyone can make a difference by starting in their own small world: learn good values, live them, and work through them in small groups. His favorite of his own books (S069).
- **Key frameworks:** transformation tables; the six pictures of transformation (me, partnership, ladder, heart, table, bridge); the possibilist; vision caster versus vision carrier.
- **Taught in sources:** S062, S064, S067, S069, S078, S102, S104, S105

## Faith and devotional

### The Maxwell Leadership Bible (2002; later editions)
- **Core thesis:** Leadership lessons placed through Scripture, about 600 of them (S094). He signs copies with the line that everything he knows about leadership he learned from this book (S066, S085, S096).
- **Taught in sources:** S061, S066, S084, S085, S094, S096

### Leadership Promises for Every Day (2003)
- **Core thesis:** A one-a-day devotional: a verse and a few lines for people who do not read much.
- **Taught in sources:** S092

### Think on These Things (1979)
- **Core thesis:** His first book, named from Philippians 4. He says it was weak and tells people not to buy it, but it started his writing (S057, S088).
- **Taught in sources:** S057, S084, S088

### Other titles he mentions once
- Breakthrough Parenting (1996), his one parenting book (S060). The Greatest Story Ever Told, a blank journal he gives students for their dreams, prayers, and acts of kindness (S105). Beyond Success is a five-lesson small-group course, not a book (S075).

## Other writers he leans on
These are other people's books; when he quotes them he is citing, not speaking in his own words.
- **The Bible:** his main leadership source. Scripture he cites and builds on most: Matthew 5 (salt and light), 1 Corinthians 9 (Paul's strategy for reaching people), Ephesians 3 (his legacy prayer), Luke 4 (the wilderness), Numbers 13 (the spies), Hebrews 11 and 12, Jonah, and Peter's denial (S084, S091, S093, S094, S096, S098, S099, S101).
- **Dale Carnegie, How to Win Friends and Influence People:** the book his father gave him; he calls it the relationship Bible (S013, S021, S060, S087).
- **Jim Collins, Good to Great:** the level five leader who blends personal humility with professional will (S010).
- **Malcolm Gladwell, The Tipping Point:** the law of the few, as he uses it for investing in a few people (S040).
- **Andy Stanley, Not in It to Win It:** Christians lose credibility by fighting culture wars; follow Jesus in loving and serving (S047).
- **Philip Yancey, The Jesus I Never Knew:** changed his life in 1995 (S086).
- **M. Scott Peck, The Road Less Traveled:** life is difficult, and accepting that makes it less hard (S093).
- **Jamie Kern Lima, Worthy and Believe It:** self-worth sets how high people rise (S011, S030).
- **Marilyn Gist, The Extraordinary Power of Leader Humility:** humility as deep regard for others' dignity (S012).
- **Doris Kearns Goodwin, Team of Rivals:** Lincoln put the best minds in the room, rivals included (S060).
- **Also mentioned:** James Clear, Atomic Habits (S060); Simon Sinek, The Infinite Game (S062); Ed Mylett, The Power of One More (S065); Elmer Towns, The Ten Largest Churches in America (S100).

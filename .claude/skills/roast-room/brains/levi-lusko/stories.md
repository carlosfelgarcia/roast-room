# Stories: Levi Lusko

19 stories and cases he reuses, merged across the nine note batches, most-used first, with the lesson he draws. Source ids in parentheses; dates are upload dates from `sources.md`.

1. **Lenya's death, December 20, 2012.** His five-year-old daughter died of an asthma attack five days before Christmas; he did CPR on her, and her corneas gave sight to two blind people, which became the book Through the Eyes of a Lion. That night Jennie sent him back into the hospital to invite the staff to the Christmas Eve service, and some came and gave their lives to Christ. Lesson: pain can become a microphone and a passport; run toward the roar, not away from it (S009, S017, S019, S020, S022, S024, S029, S043).
2. **Panic attacks at 38 (first one May 2020).** Heart near 185 to 189 at 2 a.m., Scripture and a sermon did not calm it, Jennie played a breathing app, and it took counseling, a week-long intensive, a body scan, medicine for a season, and less caffeine. Lesson: it was biological as well as spiritual; "The Bible didn't help me in my panic attacks." (S005) (S001, S003, S005, S007, S015, S016, S033).
3. **Yes to everything after grief.** After Lenya died he buried himself in work, flew to South Africa twice for a single weekend, and hit two million Delta miles; it cost him staff who could not keep up. Years later, on his book's launch day, he said no to another Africa weekend and saw that he had changed. Lesson: the pace is a credit card bill that comes due (S001, S003, S005, S011, S015, S016).
4. **The mother wound under the drive.** His parents' marriage came apart in his teens and nobody spoke of it; porn started the same year, and ministry became his source of approval. A counselor's raised eyebrow connected the two, and he says he would once have passed a polygraph that it had no effect. Lesson: look for the thing under the thing, and get someone who can see your blind spots (S003, S007, S008, S016, S018).
5. **Planting Fresh Life in Montana.** He turned down a staff job at Greg Laurie's Harvest and a permanent post at a 2,000-person church, sold his car for the moving truck, had one year of savings, and started with 14 people above a bar. He gave God five years. Lesson: obedience over a career move, and faithfulness in obscurity comes before growth (S007, S009, S014, S020).
6. **His father's death in 2024.** His dad, who taught him to fish and pray, died of pancreatic cancer a year after the diagnosis; Levi thought he was a veteran of grief after Lenya and was blindsided. Lesson: no grief punch card; every loss is a new category (S003, S005, S007, S009, S015, S030).
7. **Throwing up before every sermon.** For nearly two years as a young preacher he vomited before preaching; after his first Bible college sermon a student called him the next Billy Graham. Lesson: fear can be trained, and early praise fed an unhealthy need for validation (S001, S003, S006, S011).
8. **The empty-room sermon that paid off in 2020.** In 2009 he recorded a sermon to an empty theater on a Thursday for Sunday ("rowboat church"); the church started in 2007 with Myspace invites and Costco cameras. In 2020 the skill carried a live online service where he called viewers by name. Lesson: be early to the medium people already use (S002, S011, S012).
9. **Last Supper on the Moon, written in defiance.** After a year of fear he started it on his birthday, vowing not to put his feet on the floor until the first chapter was done, in the bedroom where he had had panic attacks: "it was almost like an act of defiance" (S033). He wrote it with no contract so it would be pure. Lesson: creative work as obedience, motive over deal (S001, S004, S015, S033).
10. **Book launches: stunts versus community.** A live boxing class and a SoulCycle event "ended up not changing anything on how many books you sold." (S027). Later launches were run clubs and seven cities in seven days, where strangers met and became friends. Lesson: build community around the message, not stunts (S016, S027, S030, S031).
11. **The email he opened against his own rule.** He checked email before time with God and the subject line "i'm disappointed in you" threw him into a tailspin. Lesson: let God's voice be first each morning (S002).
12. **Watching his kids sleep.** After a day of being short with them and on his phone, he watched his children sleep and saw what mattered; that pain started the book I Declare War. Lesson: the phone steals presence you cannot get back (S025).
13. **YouVersion, Life.Church, and giving tools away.** He and Jennie picked the same verse for their Christmas card on the same day from a shared reading plan, and he praises Life.Church for giving away free sermons, Church Online, and the Bible app his kids use. Lesson: shared reading connects people, and generous tech multiplies other ministries (S024, S025, S031).
14. **Chip Lusko and the razor blade.** His father physically cut Skip Heitzig's sermon tapes so they could be duplicated for radio, and as a teen Levi worked in the church tape room. Lesson: "he would just do anything he could to try and amplify, package, cleverly get it out there." (S032) (S009, S032).
15. **Never miss twice, over the date line.** Crossing the International Date Line on a 20-hour flight, he lost track of his Bible reading day and jokes that he blames physics. Lesson: you will miss; the danger is the second miss, so get back up (S045).
16. **Skip's undated devotional.** His mentor's 365-day devotional has no dates, so readers can start any day and a skipped day does not matter; Fresh Life will use it church-wide in 2027. Lesson (Skip's design, Levi adopting it): remove the guilt of the calendar and keep a path into the whole Bible (S032).
17. **The Ritz-Carlton Hawaii room.** A bartender heard a couple's Hawaii trip was cancelled for cancer, and the staff turned their room into Hawaii. Lesson: people come for a moment, not the product, and that couple is a customer for life (S011).
18. **The church that would not bury Lenya.** A church refused to let them bury her there because they were not members. Lesson: never let a church become inward-focused (S020).
19. **The podcast network that cost him listeners.** Launching a network sent some of his own listeners to other shows. Lesson: it is a win if your numbers fall while the others rise (S008).
